# 📊 Market Share Analysis – Project Plan

## 1. Objective

Analyze market share across product categories and time to understand competitive position and identify growth opportunities.

---

## 2. Key Questions

- What is our market share by category?
- Are we gaining or losing share over time?
- Who are the strongest competitors in each category?
- Which categories offer the best growth opportunities?

---

## 3. Dataset Overview

- **Time Period:** 2015–2018
- **Granularity:** Yearly
- **Categories:** Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology

**Key Fields:**

- Year
- Region
- Segment
- Category
- Company Sales
- Competitor A Sales
- Competitor B Sales
- Competitor C Sales
- Total Market

---

## 4. Approach

### Phase 1: Data Preparation

- Clean dataset (remove duplicates, fix dates)
- Validate key fields
- Simulate competitor sales
- Create Total Market column

---

### Phase 2: Market Size Analysis

- Calculate total market per category
- Analyze growth trends

---

### Phase 3: Market Share Analysis

- Calculate company and competitor shares
- Compare performance across categories

---

### Phase 4: Insight Generation

- Identify strong and weak categories
- Assess competitive threats

---

### Phase 5: Recommendations

- Suggest where to invest, defend, or optimize
