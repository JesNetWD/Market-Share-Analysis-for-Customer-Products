## INSIGHTS

1. Office Supplies has the largest market size with more than $338,000 worth of sales made between 2015 and 2018, Technology comes in second with about 269,000 sales and Furniture is last with about an 180,000 in sales.
2. Our company dominates the Furniture category with a market share of 40%.
3. Competitor B is the market leader in the Office Supplies category with a market share of 32% followed by Competitor A which has a 29% market share and then Our Company with a 21% market share.
4. In the Technology category Competitor B is still the market leader with a market share of 34% followed by Our Company with a market share of 31%.
5. Competitor B has a higher overall market share of about 30% followed by Our Company with an overall market share of approximately 29%, then Competitor A with an overall market share of approximately 24%, and lastly Competitor C with an overall market share of 17%.
6. In 2016 the market saw a dip of 7% across all categories, a 9% dip in Furniture sales, a 3% dip in Technology, an 11% dip in Office Supplies.
7. The Consumer segment drives the most number of sales across all companies, followed by the Corporate Segment and then the Home Office Segment.
8. The west region contributes the most at 31% to our company's overall sales. It also contributes the most to Our Company's furniture sales (34%) and our Office Supplies sales (31%). However the East contributes the most to our companies Technology sales.

## RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Furniture has the lowest market size yet Our Company dominates the Furniture Category with a market share of 40% so we should adopt a defensive strategy:
   Limit our spending to the bare minimum needed to sustain current operations and productivity and instead shift our focus to customer retention through customer loyalty programs and better customer service.

2. Office Supplies has the highest market size yet Our Company has a low market share of 21% so we should adopt a growth stategy:

- Reinvest the surplus profits from the Furniture Category into marketing and customer acquisition.
- Improve differentiation (pricing, bundles, service).
- Target high-value customer segments.

3. Our company is a Strong Number Two in the Technology Category with a market share of 31% so we should adopt a competitive strategy:

- Focus on the competitor experience to win on service and a better customer community.
- Be faster at adopting and developing new tech or responding to customer trends that Competitor B is too slow to do.
- Focus on strong-performing regions like the East to scale growth.
